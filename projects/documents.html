Problem and context

At Necton, I work on document data standardization and data architecture for AI services. Documents can express the same information differently, while similar expressions can have different meanings. I analyzed their context and organized information using consistent criteria.

My role

I participated in analyzing source-document meaning and representation differences, standardizing data, and designing structures for use and maintenance.

Approach

  1. Source-data analysis: Examined differences in document representation and information context.
  2. Standardization: Organized representations under common criteria and checked consistency.
  3. Structure design: Participated in data-structure design considering use and maintenance.

Scope

I participated in source-document analysis, representation standardization, and structure design for use and maintenance.
