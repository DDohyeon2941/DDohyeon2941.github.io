I study imbalanced learning and connect real-world data to model training and evaluation.

During my graduate studies, I investigated learning bias caused by data distributions. In industry, I validated and linked manufacturing data to build training datasets.

These experiences taught me that applying a model starts with understanding what the data means and how its records relate. I use that understanding to define problems and design training and evaluation.
