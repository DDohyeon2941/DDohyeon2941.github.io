Coursework & personal projects

  • GAN augmentation for imbalanced image data

    I explored what makes generated samples useful for minority-class classification, using class-specific generation distributions and signals from two critics.

    2019 graduate team project · 2023 personal follow-up study

  • Federated learning experiments with Flower

    Can simply aggregating models produce a good global model when clients have different data quality? Our team compared aggregation strategies in Flower and experimented with controlling updates from clients with incorrect labels.

    SeoulTech database course · Team project

  • Emergency exit sign recognition for people with visual impairments

    Our team built classifiers to recognize emergency exit signs in indoor images and converted them to TensorFlow Lite for lightweight inference.

    SeoulTech IoT course · Team project

  • OOD generalization with differences in data quality

    I tested how to adjust the IRM penalty when training environments have different levels of label noise.

    Coursework & personal projects · Course assignment
