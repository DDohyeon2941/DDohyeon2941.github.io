Projects

In industry, I validated, linked, and aligned real-world data for training and evaluation. Coursework, personal experiments, competitions, and regional analysis also show how we defined the subjects and problems alongside implementing models.

Industry projects

  • Manufacturing data validation and linkage

    Data from successive stages could not be used as valid training pairs without confirming that the records described the same target. I verified those correspondences and built a linked dataset for analysis and model training.

    Industry project · Generalized public description

  • Manufacturing time-series alignment and ML pipelines

    Records collected at different times can create misleading comparisons or expose a model to information unavailable when it makes a prediction. I aligned records around the intended prediction time and prepared consistent inputs for training and evaluation.

    Industry project · Generalized public description

Coursework & personal projects

  • GAN augmentation for imbalanced image data

    Adding more minority-class images does not guarantee that a classifier learns to distinguish that class better. Building on graduate team experiments, I explored ways to preserve class characteristics in generated images and assess their usefulness for classification.

    2019 graduate team project · 2023 personal follow-up study

  • Federated learning experiments with Flower

    Incorrect labels on some clients can weaken a shared model. Our course team compared ways to combine client models and tested controls on their updates; those controls did not improve performance in our experiments.

    SeoulTech database course · Team project

  • Emergency exit sign recognition for people with visual impairments

    Emergency exit signs can be difficult for people with visual impairments to read directly. Our team used camera images to distinguish exit signs from other objects and adapted the classifiers for lightweight inference as a technical feasibility study.

    SeoulTech IoT course · Team project

  • OOD generalization with differences in data quality

    A model can rely on patterns that stop working in a new environment, and some training environments may have less reliable labels. In a course assignment, I adjusted how strongly each environment constrained learning and built experiments to test that approach.

    Coursework & personal projects · Course assignment

Competitions & regional analysis

  • Defining closing and continuing commercial areas in Seongsu-dong

    Seongsu-dong’s popularity did not explain why some commercial areas struggled while others remained active. Our team defined closing and continuing groups within industries with relatively frequent closures, then compared their sales, customer patterns, and accessibility to propose local policy directions.

    2024.07–2024.12 · Seongdong-gu Big Data Fellowship · Team project

  • Insurance claim review classification

    Insurance claims need different levels of review, and patterns can differ by disease group. In a competition, our team built classifiers tailored to those groups to distinguish immediate payment, document review, and on-site review.

    2020.08–2020.10 · Financial Big Data Festival · Team project

Other projects

  • Document data standardization and structure design

    Different documents expressed the same information in inconsistent ways, making it difficult to interpret and reuse together. I analyzed the meaning in context, standardized equivalent representations, and helped design data structures for use and maintenance.

    Context under review · Document analysis, standardization, and structure design
