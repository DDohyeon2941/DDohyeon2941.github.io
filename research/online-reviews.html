Review satisfaction factors and international visitor volume

Investigation on the Factors Affecting the Number of International Visitors Using Online Reviews

Journal of the Korean Institute of Industrial Engineers, 2021 · Second author

We extracted satisfaction factors and sentiment scores from tourism reviews and analyzed them alongside Seoul’s short-stay foreign population estimates.

Method:
sentence topics and sentiment → area/month-level regression
Visitor-volume proxy:
Seoul’s short-stay foreign population estimates

Research problem

Beyond identifying what tourists are satisfied with, we analyzed how those factors relate to visitor volume.

Approach

Tripadvisor reviews are processed sentence by sentence, with satisfaction factors extracted using semantics-assisted NMF. An elastic-net sentiment dictionary produces factor-level sentiment scores. These are combined with weather, search, and regional information and analyzed using ridge regression.

Visitor volume is a proxy based on Seoul’s short-stay foreign population estimates aggregated by area and month.

Contributions

I participated as second author. The analysis connects review factors and sentiment with visitor volume. Because it uses observational data and a proxy measure, the results do not establish causal effects or measure the complete population of actual visitors.
