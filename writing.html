Company ML Training

I created these ML educational materials for junior colleagues at work, connecting foundational concepts with model interpretation, data validation, and evaluation. Round 1 focuses on concepts and practical interpretation; Round 2 revisits them through mathematics.

Round 1 — ML concepts and practical interpretation

  1. The ML workflow and linear regression

    Learning goal Understand what a linear model captures and how residual patterns reveal what it misses.

    The workflow from data collection to evaluation and application, regression coefficients, overfitting, multicollinearity, and interpreting R².

  2. Tree models and ensembles

    Learning goal Explain tree predictions, distinguish bagging from boosting, and interpret feature importance with its limitations.

    Decision tree splits and predictions, the differences between bagging and boosting, and feature importance.

    Supplement: MDI · OOB permutation · SHAP

    Learning goal Compare what MDI, OOB permutation, and SHAP measure using the same teaching example.

  3. Clustering and similarity

    Learning goal Choose distances and scaling suited to the data, and assess whether the resulting clusters are meaningful.

    Hard and soft clustering, choosing distances and similarity measures, the effects of scaling, and interpreting clusters.

    Supplement: Min–max scaling vs. standardization

    Learning goal Compare how min–max scaling and standardization change the same data.

  4. Metrics and model evaluation

    Learning goal Choose metrics that reflect the task and recognize failures that an overall score can hide.

    The meanings and limitations of regression and classification metrics and how to examine performance in important regions.

  5. When model results should not be trusted Draft material

    Learning goal Question apparently good results by checking leakage, bias, distribution shifts, overfitting, and target definition.

    Assessing model reliability through data leakage, bias, distribution shifts, overfitting, and target definition.

  6. Time-series data and validation Draft material

    Learning goal Identify temporal dependence and design time-based validation that avoids future-information leakage.

    Temporal dependence, data leakage, time-based validation, retraining, and input issues after deployment.

Round 2 — ML through mathematics

  1. The ML workflow and mathematical notation Draft material

    Learning goal Read ML notation and connect inputs, predictions, and loss functions to the learning process.

    Functions and mathematical notation, the relationship between inputs and predictions, and learning through loss functions.

  2. The mathematics of linear regression

    Learning goal Use equations to explain regression coefficients, R², significance tests, and multicollinearity.

    Calculating regression coefficients, decomposing R², t/F tests, and multicollinearity.

  3. The mathematics of trees and ensembles

    Learning goal Connect impurity and error reduction to tree splits, and explain how bagging and boosting combine models.

    Gini and entropy, classification and regression split criteria, feature importance, and the calculations behind bagging and boosting.
